## Interpretation

The defensible statement is:

> Given the accepted TX1 anchor, planar geometry, 1-foot radial prior, and the six retained
> 2.4 GHz phase profiles, TX2 most likely lies in the board's lower-right sector, approximately
> 19–35 degrees from +x. The data do not support a centimetre-level absolute coordinate.

Important unresolved effects are antenna phase-centre and pattern error, mutual coupling among
the closely spaced receive whips, uncalibrated switch/launch group delay, room multipath, TX1
anchor error, and the imposed radial prior. The next accuracy step is an in-situ complex array
calibration at surveyed source positions—not more repeats of an unchanged geometry.
